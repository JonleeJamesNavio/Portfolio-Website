I'm an Automation & AI Systems Specialist who builds the infrastructure businesses run on: CRM architecture, data pipelines, and AI-powered workflows built to keep running reliably, with error handling, monitoring, and documentation behind them.

My work centers on three things. Designing automation systems across platforms like GoHighLevel, n8n, Make, and Zapier, and extending them with Google Apps Script and custom code where no-code tools reach their limits. Building ETL pipelines that turn messy raw data into something a business can actually act on. And integrating LLMs where they earn their place, classifying, structuring, and automating decisions that used to eat up staff hours. I care less about which tool is trendy and more about whether the system still works six months from now without me babysitting it.
